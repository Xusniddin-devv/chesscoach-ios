Chess Coach

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 24, 2026

Chess Coach is an iPhone app made by Xusniddin Qalandarov ("I", "me"). This page explains what the app does with your information. The short version: I don't collect your personal data, there are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your iPhone

Your games, analyses, puzzles, lessons, streak and training plan are stored on your device. The chess engine (Stockfish) runs on your iPhone, so your games are analysed without being sent anywhere.

iCloud

If you're signed in to iCloud, the app syncs your progress through your own private iCloud database so it appears on your other devices. Apple stores this data under your Apple Account. I can't see or access it.

chess.com and Lichess

If you enter a chess.com or Lichess username, the app downloads that player's public games from chess.com or Lichess to analyse them. Only the username is sent, and only to those two services. Their own privacy policies apply to those requests.

Voice moves

If you hold the mic button to say a move, speech is recognised on your iPhone. Audio isn't recorded, stored or sent anywhere.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. I never see your payment details. The app uses RevenueCat to confirm whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat receives your purchase history and an anonymous ID created by the app, not your name or email. This information is used only so the app works. It isn't used for tracking or advertising. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Notifications

If you start a free trial and allow notifications, the app schedules a reminder on your iPhone before the trial ends. It's a local notification: nothing is sent to a server.

Children

The app doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes the data on your iPhone. To remove synced data, go to Settings, then your name, then iCloud, then Manage Account Storage, and delete Chess Coach's data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
