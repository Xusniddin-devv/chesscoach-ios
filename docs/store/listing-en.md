## Description [4000]
Chess Coach studies the games you already play and turns your mistakes into a daily plan.

IMPORT YOUR GAMES
Enter your chess.com or Lichess username. Your recent games are analysed in seconds, and the coach shows the moments that cost you the most.

A COACH THAT EXPLAINS
Play bots rated from 600 to 2300. In Training mode the coach stops you after a mistake, tells you what you missed, and lets you take it back and try again. Or play Silent mode and review the game after.

YOUR MISTAKES BECOME PUZZLES
Every blunder you make becomes a puzzle from your own games, so you practise exactly what you keep getting wrong.

FULL GAME REVIEW
Every move gets a grade, from brilliant to blunder, with the best move and the line behind it. Try your own ideas on the board.

A PLAN FOR EACH DAY
Puzzles, lessons and a game, picked for your level, with a streak to keep you going.

LESSONS FROM ZERO
Checkmates, openings and endgames, step by step.

POWERED BY STOCKFISH 18, ON YOUR IPHONE
The world's strongest chess engine runs on your phone. Analysis works offline.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No ads and no tracking. Your games stay on your device, never on our servers.

OPEN SOURCE
Chess Coach is free software under the GPLv3. The source code is on GitHub.

Get everything with Coach Pro, billed yearly or monthly. Payment is charged to your Apple Account. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel them in your Apple Account settings.
