Get help

Found a bug or have a question? Open an issue on GitHub. Include your iPhone model and iOS version if something isn't working.

Common questions

How do I cancel my subscription?
Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Chess Coach.

I bought Coach Pro but the app doesn't show it.
Open the paywall and tap Restore. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.

My games aren't on my other iPhone.
Both devices need to be signed in to the same iCloud account with iCloud Drive turned on. Syncing can take a few minutes.

Can I get a refund?
Refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
